# Japanese Miso Ramen

Cuisine: Japanese
Time: 45 minutes
Serves: 2

## Ingredients

### Broth
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp white miso paste
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1-inch piece ginger, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1/2 tsp chili paste (optional)

### Toppings
- 200g ramen noodles (fresh or dried)
- 2 soft-boiled eggs (marinated in soy sauce and mirin for 30 min)
- 150g pork belly or tofu (for vegetarian)
- 1 cup baby spinach
- 4 shiitake mushrooms, sliced and sautéed
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1 sheet nori, cut into strips
- 2 tbsp corn kernels
- Sesame seeds and sesame oil for garnish

## Instructions

1. **Marinate eggs:** Soft-boil eggs (6.5 minutes), cool in ice bath, peel. Marinate in 3 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tbsp mirin + water to cover, for at least 30 minutes.
2. In a pot, combine stock, ginger, and garlic. Bring to a simmer and cook 15 minutes. Strain out solids.
3. Whisk in miso paste, soy sauce, mirin, and sesame oil. Taste and adjust seasoning. Keep warm.
4. Cook ramen noodles according to package directions. Drain and rinse.
5. If using pork belly: slice and sear in a pan over high heat until caramelized. If using tofu: cube and pan-fry until golden.
6. **Assemble bowls:** Divide noodles between 2 deep bowls. Ladle hot broth over noodles.
7. Top with pork/tofu, halved marinated egg, mushrooms, spinach, corn, nori, and green onions.
8. Drizzle sesame oil and sprinkle sesame seeds. Serve immediately.

## Vegetarian Adaptation
- Use vegetable stock instead of chicken stock
- Replace pork belly with firm tofu or pan-fried mushrooms
- Check miso paste is vegan (most white miso is)
