# Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)

Cuisine: Indian
Time: 50 minutes
Serves: 4
Diet tags: gluten-free

## Ingredients

### For the Marinade
- 500g boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt to taste

### For the Sauce
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1-inch piece ginger, grated
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 400g crushed tomatoes (canned)
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

## Instructions

1. Mix all marinade ingredients with chicken. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (overnight is best).
2. Grill or pan-fry marinated chicken over medium-high heat for 8-10 minutes until lightly charred. Set aside.
3. Heat butter and oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let sizzle for 30 seconds.
4. Add onion and sauté until golden brown, about 8 minutes.
5. Add garlic and ginger; cook 2 minutes until fragrant.
6. Stir in coriander, garam masala, and chili powder. Cook 1 minute.
7. Add crushed tomatoes, stir well, and simmer 15 minutes until sauce thickens.
8. Let sauce cool slightly, then blend until smooth. Return to pan.
9. Add cooked chicken, cream, and sugar. Simmer 10 minutes.
10. Season with salt and pepper. Garnish with cilantro. Serve with naan or basmati rice.
