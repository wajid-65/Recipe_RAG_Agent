# Keto Avocado Egg Salad

Cuisine: American
Time: 15 minutes
Serves: 2
Diet tags: keto, low-carb, gluten-free, dairy-free

## Ingredients
- 4 large hard-boiled eggs, peeled and chopped
- 1 large ripe avocado, mashed
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise (avocado oil mayo preferred)
- 1 celery stalk, finely diced
- 2 tbsp red onion, finely diced
- 1 tbsp fresh dill or parsley, chopped
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Paprika for garnish
- Lettuce leaves or low-carb wraps to serve

## Instructions

1. Hard-boil eggs: place in cold water, bring to boil, turn off heat, cover for 10 minutes, then transfer to ice bath. Peel and chop.
2. Halve avocado, remove pit, scoop flesh into a bowl, and mash with a fork until mostly smooth (leave some chunks for texture).
3. Add lemon juice to avocado immediately to prevent browning.
4. Mix in mustard and mayonnaise until combined.
5. Fold in chopped eggs, celery, and red onion.
6. Add fresh dill or parsley. Season generously with salt and pepper.
7. Serve in lettuce cups or on low-carb wraps. Sprinkle with paprika.

## Nutrition (per serving)
Calories: ~380 | Fat: 32g | Protein: 14g | Net Carbs: 4g

## Substitutions
- No mayo: replace with Greek yogurt (adds ~1g carb)
- No dill: use chives or tarragon
- Add crunch: mix in diced cucumber or bacon crumbles
