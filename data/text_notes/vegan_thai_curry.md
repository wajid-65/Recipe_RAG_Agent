# Vegan Thai Green Curry

Cuisine: Thai
Time: 30 minutes
Serves: 4
Diet: vegan, gluten-free

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp green curry paste
- 1 can (400ml) coconut milk
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1 block firm tofu, cubed
- 1 cup broccoli florets
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (use tamari for gluten-free)
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- Fresh basil leaves
- Cooked jasmine rice, to serve

## Instructions
1. Heat curry paste in a pan over medium heat for 1 minute until fragrant.
2. Add coconut milk and vegetable broth, stir to combine.
3. Add tofu, broccoli, and bell pepper. Simmer for 10-12 minutes.
4. Stir in soy sauce and brown sugar.
5. Top with fresh basil and serve over jasmine rice.
