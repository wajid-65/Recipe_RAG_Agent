# Classic Chocolate Cake

Cuisine: American
Time: 45 minutes
Serves: 8

## Ingredients
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup cocoa powder
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup hot water

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350F (175C). Grease two 9-inch cake pans.
2. Whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
3. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla. Mix for 2 minutes.
4. Stir in hot water until batter is thin and smooth.
5. Pour into pans and bake for 30-35 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
6. Cool for 10 minutes before removing from pans.
