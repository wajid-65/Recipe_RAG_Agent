# Vegan Black Bean Tacos

Cuisine: Mexican
Time: 20 minutes
Serves: 4
Diet tags: vegan, vegetarian, dairy-free, gluten-free

## Ingredients

### For the Black Beans
- 2 cans (400g each) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Juice of 1 lime

### For the Toppings
- 8 small corn tortillas (gluten-free)
- 1 cup purple cabbage, shredded
- 2 ripe avocados, sliced
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 cup red onion, finely diced
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 jalapeño, sliced (optional)
- Lime wedges for serving

### For the Chipotle Sauce (optional)
- 1/2 cup cashews, soaked 2 hours, drained
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo sauce
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. **Make chipotle sauce (optional):** Blend soaked cashews, chipotle pepper, water, lime juice, and salt until completely smooth. Set aside.
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and cook 1 minute.
3. Add black beans, cumin, smoked paprika, and chili powder. Stir and cook 5 minutes until heated through.
4. Mash about half the beans with a fork for a mix of whole and creamy. Add lime juice and season with salt and pepper.
5. Warm corn tortillas in a dry skillet or directly over a gas flame for 30 seconds per side.
6. **Assemble tacos:** Spoon bean mixture into tortillas. Top with cabbage, avocado, tomatoes, red onion, jalapeño, and cilantro.
7. Drizzle chipotle sauce over the top. Serve with lime wedges.
