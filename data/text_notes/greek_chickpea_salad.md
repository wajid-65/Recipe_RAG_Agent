# Greek Chickpea Salad (Revithosalata)

Cuisine: Greek
Time: 15 minutes
Serves: 4
Diet tags: vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, high-protein

## Ingredients
- 2 cans (400g each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 English cucumber, diced
- 200g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup Kalamata olives, pitted
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 2 tbsp fresh mint, chopped

### Dressing
- 4 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Zest of 1 lemon + 1 tbsp lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Combine all dressing ingredients in a small jar. Shake well and let sit 5 minutes.
2. In a large bowl, combine chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, olives, parsley, and mint.
3. Pour dressing over salad and toss gently to coat everything evenly.
4. Let sit 10 minutes before serving so flavors meld.
5. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, or vinegar to your liking.
6. Serve at room temperature or chilled. Keeps refrigerated for up to 2 days.

## Additions
- Add crumbled feta cheese if not vegan (adds richness)
- Add roasted red peppers for sweetness
- Serve over mixed greens for a more substantial meal
- Add a few scoops of hummus on the side

## Nutrition (per serving)
Calories: ~290 | Protein: 11g | Carbs: 32g | Fat: 13g | Fiber: 9g
